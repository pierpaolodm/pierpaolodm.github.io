Pierpaolo Della Monica

dellamonica@diag.uniroma1.it

Ciao! I’m Pierpaolo Della Monica, a PhD student in Cybersecurity at Sapienza University of Rome, where I am lucky enough to be supervised by Ivan Visconti.

My research is on cryptography both from a theoretical and applied perspective, with a focus on the design and analysis of cryptographic protocols, mainly in the area of advanced signatures schemes and their applications in privacy-enhancing technologies such as anonymous credentials. I am also interested in the interconnection between cryptography and blockchain where I find the latter as an extremely cool playground for the former.

You can find a list of my works on my publications page.

I was born in Vietri sul Mare, a small town on the Amalfi Coast (Italy). Currently I am based in Bochum (Germany) where I am a visiting Julian Loss at Ruhr University.

My music taste is all over the place, but if I had to crown one favorite artist, it’d be Joji. My surname is “Della Monica”, not “Monica”. It happens more often than you’d think.
